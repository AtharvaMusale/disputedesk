# MKT-02: Non-delivery, cancellation and conflicting evidence
Track: marketplace

- A cancelled order that was charged: full refund.
- An order not delivered (including 'unavailable') 7 or more days after its estimated delivery date: full refund.
- Customer says the order never arrived but the carrier shows it delivered: deny.
  Exception: if the customer also left a review of 4+ stars on that order, the statements conflict: escalate for manual review.
