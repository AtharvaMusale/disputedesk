# FIN-03: Compelling evidence (Visa-style)
Track: fintech

A dispute is rebutted when at least 2 earlier undisputed transactions by the same customer, each dated 120 to 365 days before the disputed one, each match it on at least 2 of: IP address, device ID, merchant (at least one matched element must be IP or device ID, which holds automatically with these three elements). Result: deny.
Conflict: if compelling evidence exists but the card was reported lost or stolen before the disputed transaction, the evidence conflicts: escalate.
