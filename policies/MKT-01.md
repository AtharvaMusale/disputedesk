# MKT-01: Late delivery
Track: marketplace

A delivered order that arrived after its estimated delivery date is compensated by days late:
- Under 3 days late (grace period): no refund.
- 3 to under 10 days late: refund the shipping (freight) fee only (partial refund). If the shipping fee is zero there is nothing to refund.
- 10 or more days late: full refund of the amount paid.
- A late-delivery claim on an order delivered on or before the estimated date is denied.
MKT-04 (seller failure) and MKT-05 (new customers) override these tiers. See GEN-02.
