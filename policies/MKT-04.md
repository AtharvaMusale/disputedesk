# MKT-04: Seller systemic failure
Track: marketplace

A seller is in systemic failure when 20% or more of its delivered orders arrived after the estimated delivery date, over at least 20 delivered orders. Only deliveries before the claim date count.
Any late claim (1 or more days late) on a delivered order from such a seller gets a full refund. This overrides the grace period and the freight-only tier.
